Our story

From a twelve-acre plot in Ratnagiri to eighteen thousand kitchens

AgriFresh began in 2016 when three families pooled their mango orchards and a single pickup truck. We were tired of watching good produce get blended into anonymity at the mandi — so we started packing it ourselves, under our own name, and shipping it straight to the people who eat it.

Today we work with 240 smallholder farms across eleven states. Every lot is traceable to the village it came from, every price is agreed before sowing, and every farmer is paid within seven days of dispatch — not sixty.

  • Fixed, pre-agreed prices so growers can plan a season
  • No chemical ripening, polishing, colouring or blending
  • Lab residue reports available for every lot on request
  • Reusable jute and tin packaging on bulk orders
